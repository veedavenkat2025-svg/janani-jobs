# 🚀 Janani Jobs - Play Store Launch Checklist

Your app is now technically ready! Follow these simple steps to go live.

### STEP 1: The App File
In Android Studio (where you are now):
1. Click **Build** (top menu).
2. Click **Generate Signed Bundle / APK**.
3. Select **Android App Bundle** -> Click **Next**.
4. Click **Create New** (for Key store).
   - Save it as `janani_key.jks` in your Documents.
   - Use a password you will remember (e.g., `Janani2024`).
5. Click **Next** -> Select **release** -> Click **Finish**.
6. A popup will appear. Click **Locate**. This `.aab` file is what you upload to Google.

### STEP 2: The Google Account
1. Go to: [play.google.com/console](https://play.google.com/console)
2. Sign in and pay the **$25 fee**.
3. Verify your ID (Aadhar/Passport/DL).

### STEP 3: The Upload
1. In the Google Console, click **Create App**.
2. Upload the `.aab` file you found in Step 1.
3. Submit for review!
